// FRIENDS

This is a /friends page, inspired by slashfriends.org and Nick Gray.

A non-exhaustive list of people I like and admire. If we know each other and your name isn't here, blame my memory and send me a message so I can fix it.

People I met #

  • Ana Neri, a super talented programmer and community lead, and one of the most determined people I know. If she thinks about doing something, she does it.
  • Erick Wendel, one of my oldest friends and a long-time inspiration. He makes the most of every chance to make his dreams come true, something I have yet to learn.
  • Felipe Trevisan, a once-in-a-lifetime person. A focused developer and a better manager than most I've had, who tells hard truths in a way people understand.
  • Marcelo Pecin, another awesome Tibia friend. We never met in person, but it feels like we have. He's always seeing the positive side of things and cheering everyone up. One of those people that you feel super good to be around. An amazing developer, persistent and determined on his goals.
  • Oscar Marion, we met through Felipe and clicked right away over too many shared hobbies. I admire how he gets himself heard without hurting anyone's feelings.
  • Ranieri Althoff, a friendship born from us liking the same shitty game called Tibia. One of the people I learn the most with, especially low level stuff too complex for my brain.
  • Raphael Amorim, tells you smart things you only half understand, then comes back with a working programming language, terminal or bundler. The most prolific programmer I know.
  • Robin Pokorný, we worked together for a while and I keep coming back to a few of his articles that really shaped my thinking.
  • Roz, one of my oldest dev friends, he taught me to think pragmatically about software. Avid OSS contributor and one of the most talented developers I know.
  • Wellington Almeida is probably one of the most positive persons I've ever met. He's super chill, knows a lot a bout development, especially in the front-end, and is always willing to help. I admire his ability to stay calm and collected in any situation, and his dedication to his craft.
  • Weslley Araújo one of the most competent programmers I've ever met. An amazing full-stack developer that really has a passion for what he does. Plus, he's a maintainer of a lot of very big open source projects and loves to do it!
  • William Oliveira, introduced me to tech communities and pushed me to write and do open source. If you are reading this, it's mostly because of him.

People I haven't met, but admire #

  • Addy Osmani, I've always admired his writing style, and recently came to like his books on philosophy as well.
  • Axel Rauschmayer, Ph.D, his essays and books inspire me and I read everything he writes. He made me realize I could write about programming and be taken seriously.
  • Cyrus Lopez, extremely insightful essays and good taste in UIs. My blog design is based on his.
  • Dylan Beattie, his talk The Art of Code is still the most interesting, funny, and inspiring talk I've ever seen. I want to be like him when I grow up.
  • Lydia Hallie, helped me a lot to visualize how I could make good content.
  • Martin Kleppmann, his book Designing Data-Intensive Applications is one of the best I've read, and he kindly answered all my emails when I tried to write about it.
  • Sean Goedecke, I love his insights on being a software engineer and how he navigates it.
  • Simon Späti, absurd insights and a super interesting site with a second brain, I love the content around it.
  • swyx, helped me a lot setting up my learn in public style.
  • Tanya Reilly, her book The Staff Engineer's Path has been a great inspiration for me since I became a staff engineer.
  • マリウス, hard to find online, but one of the few in my RSS feed. Their blog has it all, from luggage to books to software, and I want that organization someday.
