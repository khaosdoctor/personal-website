// FRIENDS

A non-exhaustive list of people I like and admire, an old-school blogroll that keeps growing as I remember people. If we know each other and your name isn't here, blame my memory and send me a message so I can fix it.

This is a /friends page, inspired by slashfriends.org and Nick Gray.

People I met

  • Ana Neri, a super talented programmer and community lead, and probably one of the most determined people I know. If she can think about doing something, she will probably do it.
  • Erick Wendel, one of my oldest friends and an inspiration for many years. His ability to make the most of every opportunity and make his dreams come true, no matter what happens, is something I have yet to learn.
  • Felipe Trevisan, one of those people you meet once in a lifetime. A focused developer with management skills that make most managers I ever had look like kids, and he can tell hard truths in a way people understand.
  • Marcelo Pecin, another friend I met playing Tibia. Funny enough, we never met in person, but it feels like we have. An amazing developer, super persistent on his projects and determined on his goals.
  • Oscar Marion, we met through Felipe and clicked right away, same interests and too many hobbies to count. We worked together for a while and I admire his ability to get himself heard without hurting anyone's feelings.
  • Ranieri Althoff, a friendship made by accident because we both play the same shitty game, Tibia. One of the people I learn the most with, especially low level programming and the stuff too complicated for my small brain.
  • Raphael Amorim, pops up, tells you a lot of very smart things you don't understand half of, then comes back with a fully working compiler, terminal or packer. The most prolific programmer I've ever known.
  • Robin Pokorný, we worked together for a while and I keep coming back to a few of his articles that shaped my thinking.
  • Roz, one of my oldest friends in development and the one who taught me how to think pragmatically about software. An avid OSS contributor and one of the most talented developers I've ever met.
  • William Oliveira, the person who introduced me to tech communities and pushed me to start writing and doing open source content. If you are reading this, it's mostly because of him.

People I haven't met, but admire

  • Addy Osmani, I've always admired his writing style, and recently came to like his books on philosophy as well.
  • Cyrus Lopez, never met him, but his essays are extremely insightful and he has good taste in UIs. My blog design is based on his.
  • Lydia Hallie, helped me a lot to visualize how I could make good content.
  • Martin Kleppmann, his book Designing Data-Intensive Applications is one of the best software books I've ever read, and he was kind enough to answer all my emails and research questions when I tried to write about it.
  • Sean Goedecke, I love his insights on being a software engineer and how he navigates it.
  • Simon Späti, absurd insights and a super interesting site with a second brain, I love the content around it.
  • swyx, helped me a lot setting up my learn in public style.
  • マリウス, extremely difficult to find online, but one of the few people I keep in my RSS feed. I also want to have that level of organization in my life someday (their blog has it all, from luggages to books to software).
