## Description

The multimeter on my electronics bench, for checking voltages, continuity and whether a circuit is doing what it should. I think this is the most common multimeter in the world.

## Impressions

It does what it should and has a pretty okay battery, running on a pair of AAA batteries, I think. The only problem with it is that it has a bolt on the backside, so you have to unscrew it to change the batteries, which is terrible, but okay.

The readings are pretty precise and the test leads that come with it are pretty nice. I bought a bunch of other test leads as well to use with it, but I usually use just two of them. It's a pretty okay multimeter, it does all the things that I need it to do.
