## Description

The tuner on my pedalboard. It's the first pedal so the signal path starts there, like it usually does. It's in a pretty rough shape, but these Boss pedals are tanks, so it is indeed working perfectly.

## Impressions

This pedal is pretty precise, compared to the [[Donner DT-1 Tuner]] it's a _lot_ bigger, but it also has two things that I love about it:

1. A _bypass_ out jack that I can connect directly to some other pedal or send/return, making this guy also somewhat of a switcher
2. It can power itself and another pedal, today it's the [[Boss PW-3 Wah]] but it could be anything, which leaves one extra output in the [[Lekato Pedalboard]]

This alone beats everything else. It has some extra modes, so you can tune directly in chromatic mode, guitar/bass, etc but I find this really useless.

However, the size is a big problem for it. Since the pedalboard is quite small, it's something I have to take a great care, but highly recommend as a tuner pedal, even though it's _basically the same_ as **every other** tuner pedal out there.
