## People I met

- [Roz](https://github.com/roziscoding), one of my oldest dev friends, he taught me to think pragmatically about software. Avid OSS contributor and one of the most talented developers I know.
- [Ranieri Althoff](https://rsa.sh), a friendship born from the same shitty game, [Tibia](https://www.tibia.com). One of the people I learn the most with, especially low level stuff too complex for my brain.
- [Marcelo Pecin](https://www.linkedin.com/in/celopeccin/), another [Tibia](https://www.tibia.com) friend. We never met in person, but it feels like we have. An amazing developer, persistent and determined on his goals.
- [Erick Wendel](https://erickwendel.com), one of my oldest friends and a long-time inspiration. He makes the most of every chance to make his dreams come true, something I have yet to learn.
- [Oscar Marion](https://osr.im), we met through Felipe and clicked right away over too many shared hobbies. I admire how he gets himself heard without hurting anyone's feelings.
- [Felipe Trevisan](https://www.linkedin.com/in/felipe-trevisan/), a once-in-a-lifetime person. A focused developer and a better manager than most I've had, who tells hard truths in a way people understand.
- [William Oliveira](https://woliveiras.com), introduced me to tech communities and pushed me to write and do open source. If you are reading this, it's mostly because of him.
- [Raphael Amorim](https://rapha.land), tells you smart things you only half understand, then comes back with a working [compiler](https://jamlang.org), [terminal](https://rioterm.com) or [packer](https://github.com/raphamorim/oj). The most prolific programmer I know.
- [Robin Pokorný](https://robinpokorny.com), we worked together for a while and I keep coming back to a few of his articles that shaped my thinking.
- [Ana Neri](https://www.linkedin.com/in/ananeridev/), a super talented programmer and community lead, and one of the most determined people I know. If she thinks about doing something, she does it.

- [Cyrus Lopez](https://unix.foo), extremely insightful essays and good taste in UIs. My blog design is based on his.
- [swyx](https://swyx.io), helped me a lot setting up my learn in public style.
- [Simon Späti](https://www.ssp.sh), absurd insights and a super interesting site with a second brain, I love the content around it.

- [Addy Osmani](https://addyosmani.com), I've always admired his writing style, and recently came to like his books on philosophy as well.
- [Lydia Hallie](https://www.linkedin.com/in/lydia-hallie/), helped me a lot to visualize how I could make good content.
- [Martin Kleppmann](https://martin.kleppmann.com), his book [Designing Data-Intensive Applications](https://dataintensive.net) is one of the best I've read, and he kindly answered all my emails when I tried to write about it.
- [Tanya Reilly](https://www.noidea.dog), her book [The Staff Engineer's Path](https://noidea.dog/staff) has been a great inspiration for me since I became a staff engineer.
- [マリウス](https://xn--gckvb8fzb.com), hard to find online, but one of the few in my RSS feed. Their blog has it all, from luggage to books to software, and I want that organization someday.
