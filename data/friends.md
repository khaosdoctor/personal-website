## People I met

- [Roz](https://github.com/roziscoding), one of my oldest friends in development and the one who taught me how to think pragmatically about software. An avid OSS contributor and one of the most talented developers I've ever met.
- [Ranieri Althoff](https://rsa.sh), a friendship made by accident because we both play the same shitty game, [Tibia](https://www.tibia.com). One of the people I learn the most with, especially low level programming and the stuff too complicated for my small brain.
- [Marcelo Pecin](https://www.linkedin.com/in/celopeccin/), another friend I met playing [Tibia](https://www.tibia.com). Funny enough, we never met in person, but it feels like we have. An amazing developer, super persistent on his projects and determined on his goals.
- [Erick Wendel](https://erickwendel.com), one of my oldest friends and an inspiration for many years. His ability to make the most of every opportunity and make his dreams come true, no matter what happens, is something I have yet to learn.
- [Oscar Marion](https://osr.im), we met through Felipe and clicked right away, same interests and too many hobbies to count. We worked together for a while and I admire his ability to get himself heard without hurting anyone's feelings.
- [Felipe Trevisan](https://www.linkedin.com/in/felipe-trevisan/), one of those people you meet once in a lifetime. A focused developer with management skills that make most managers I ever had look like kids, and he can tell hard truths in a way people understand.
- [William Oliveira](https://woliveiras.com), the person who introduced me to tech communities and pushed me to start writing and doing open source content. If you are reading this, it's mostly because of him.
- [Raphael Amorim](https://rapha.land), pops up, tells you a lot of very smart things you don't understand half of, then comes back with a fully working [compiler](https://jamlang.org), [terminal](https://rioterm.com) or [packer](https://github.com/raphamorim/oj). The most prolific programmer I've ever known.
- [Robin Pokorný](https://robinpokorny.com), we worked together for a while and I keep coming back to a few of his articles that shaped my thinking.
- [Ana Neri](https://www.linkedin.com/in/ananeridev/), a super talented programmer and community lead, and probably one of the most determined people I know. If she can think about doing something, she will probably do it.
