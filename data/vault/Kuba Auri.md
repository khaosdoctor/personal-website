## Description

Kuba's first wired in-ear. Kuba is a Brazilian company, and the Auri came out in August [[2026]]. I got it because everyone said good things about it on Reddit, and being Brazilian, I wanted to test a Brazilian brand.

The DAC, amp and DSP are inside the USB-C plug, so the sound curve is saved in the cable and goes with it to any device, which is both a problem and a cool thing. There are 11 curves curated by Mind the Headphone (the owner, and a YouTube channel), switched through a web equalizer. The default "Auri" curve is tuned on the JM-1 target and adjusted with a survey of Kuba's customers, I personally don't like it.

A nice thing is that every part (each side, the cable, the tips) can be bought separately, so it's easy to replace and repair.

## Impressions

I use it mostly with my phone, but also with pretty much everything else (the joys of having USB-C). I tested every curve and liked "Analytical" and "Harman IE 2019" the most, Harman is the one I'm using now. I didn't like the default Auri curve, it's not bright enough for me. I like the voice to stand out and everything in the song to be clear.

Compared to the [[KZ ZSN Pro X]] it's REALLY different. The sound is way better and the isolation is better too. Honestly, it's one of the best IEMs I've had. It's super comfortable, and it fits well, at least in my ears.

The construction is where I think it has some small quirks I wasn't expecting, at least not for an IEM at this price range:

- The left and right markings are extremely hard to find, both on the IEMs and on the cable. I had to paint them red and blue. If they were any other IEMs I could use any side of the cable with any side of the drives since they fit only on one ear, but in this case, both the cables and the IEMs need to match, which makes it extra hard
- The cable is sticky and short, so it's hard to use at the gym or anything like that. I wish they were just 10cm longer
## Final thoughts

The 9 is basically for the sound, not for the construction. Sound-wise it's one of the best IEMs I've had; build-wise, I expected more.
