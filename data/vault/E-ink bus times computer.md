# E-ink pet project to have bus times and tasks in an e-ink screen

## Overview

Good project to learn how to work with ESP32 and hardware using [[E-Ink]] screens. The goal here is to learn how to work with systems that need to use an _exceptionally small_ amount of energy.

## Design

- Small Eink screen to show bus and subway departure times in nearby stations
- Show tasks I have to do and today's calendar
