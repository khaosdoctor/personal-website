## Description

A tremolo pedal I built from a kit. It was the last kit left to build from the store, and I wanted to train more builds. I already knew the store from the [[Klon Centaur Clone|Klon]] and the [[DIY Digital Tape Delay|delay]], and that's the only reason I picked this one.

It's based on the EA 68 tremolo circuit by Anthony Leo. It's fully analog and doesn't use an optocoupler, and it goes after the old Fender amp tremolo, with a slightly uneven pulse. It has three knobs for Volume, Speed and Depth.

## Impressions

Right now I don't use it a lot, because I can barely hear the difference between having it on or off. The effect is very, very faint, which is why it's a 5 and not higher. I don't know if I did something wrong in the build, but if I were designing it I would have given the tremolo a lot more room to swing.

## Final thoughts

The build itself was the fun part, especially because I accidentally swapped the knobs for volume and depth. It took me a while to notice that a 500k knob and a 20k one had quite a difference, so basically the tremolo became a distortion pedal. Super nice, by the way, but still, not a tremolo.
