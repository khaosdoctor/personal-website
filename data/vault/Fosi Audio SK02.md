## Description

The headphone amp on the [[Gaming PC]]. It's basically a glorified volume control, and it has a balanced output, which I also got a cable for.

It's a single CNC-machined aluminium body with one big knob that handles power, play/pause and volume, plus buttons for the gain and input modes. It's driverless over USB, and the line out lets it work as a preamp for active speakers or a power amp too.

## Impressions

Needed to get one of those just because I didn't want to have my [[Hifiman Sundara|Sundara]] connected to the [[Blue Yeti|Yeti]] monitor, because the monitor also relays all the audio from the microphone (after all, it's a _monitor_).

I tried to input this into the [[DeepCool CH560 Digital]] audio output, but I think I did something wrong and I am getting some noise that makes it impossible to use it there. So I needed some external DAC amplifier to control it (and getting a real knob). I had a few requirements for it:

- The Amplifier needed to be small
- It needed to have one input for both the USB and the DC in
- It needed to have both a balanced output and a normal 3.5mm jack
- Analog knob

There was another product I have viewed but this was the better option on the same price range. It's a very small block, I am pretty sure that I could also drill a hole in my table to put this through it, that would be very cool, but too much work.

The construction of it super nice, all metal, the knob is also very comfortable and it has a neat feature that you can press it to play/pause, double press to mute and long press to turn it on or off, it also has some [[LEDs]] to indicate the sampling rate but that's rather useless.

## Final thoughts

I am using it with a balanced output cable, haven't got much difference from the original using the Yeti to be honest, I had a slighter higher volume because of the power it can deliver through the cable, but that's basically it. Overall it's a good product, but nothing super special about it. I just think it's quite expensive for what it is.
