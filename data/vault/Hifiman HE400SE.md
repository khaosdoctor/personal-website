## Description

HIFIMAN's entry-level open-back planar magnetic headphones. The international version came out in [[2021]] with HIFIMAN's "stealth magnets", shaped so sound passes through them with less interference, and at launch it was the most affordable headphone HIFIMAN had made.

I couldn't find a [[Frequency Response Chart|Frequency Response]] chart from SoundGuys to make it standard, but I got one from Bad Guy:

![[graph 1.png]]

It has a good base response, and it's quite bright. But I also had to [[Equalizer (audio effect)|EQ]] it:

```txt
Preamp: -5.6 dB
Filter 1: ON LSC Fc 105 Hz Gain 5.8 dB Q 0.70
Filter 2: ON PK Fc 301 Hz Gain -1.6 dB Q 0.18
Filter 3: ON PK Fc 1975 Hz Gain 5.4 dB Q 1.84
Filter 4: ON PK Fc 2837 Hz Gain -3.2 dB Q 3.31
```

## Impressions

This is the poor cousing of [[Hifiman Sundara|Sundara]]. It delivers, like, 70% of what Sundara delivers, but it looks really cheap... So basically the problem here is a bunch of design choices that I, personally, wouldn't have done.

So, the good parts. The sound is really something, it's lighter than the Sundara (definitely because it's all plastic), it's quite comfortable, the cups are bigger and it's nice because my ears actually fit on it.

The bad parts are quite a few. First, it's all plastic, which I really don't like. The band is fixed, differently from the Sundara that has a headband on the top which lowers the weight of the headphone
