Description #

My daily watch. I bought it together with the iPad Air and the AirPods Pro, and I mostly use it for notifications and timers, plus the usual health tracking.

Impressions #

My first impression when I bought it was that it's a good clock, and it has a lot of functions that I like, like responding to messages directly on the watch and even listening to music on it. When I bought it the battery was okay. Now, five years later, I have to charge it every day, otherwise it will just last for a few hours, and it's quite bad. It's almost the same as my iPhone.

What I actually use it for is not sports or anything. It is mostly because I would like to keep sleep tracking, and this is the only way I can rely on it. It also has the timer and the alarm, which is something I really like to use, and it's one of the few things that can wake me up when I am asleep. Other than that, I mostly ignore everything else.
