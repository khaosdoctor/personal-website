Description #

The power supply of my pedalboard, it powers every pedal from a single unit. I also use its USB output for small gadgets.

Impressions #

It's a very budget pedalboard. Does what it needs to do, has a built in power supply with isolated outputs (which is extremely important for noise), all for a very interesting price. However, it's also small and doesn't come with any native way of organizing the cables other than zip ties.

Final thoughts #

It's an average thing. I know that I am most likely asking too much of it by putting 12 different pedals on top of it, but it does the job. If I had known before, I would have bought a bigger one, now I have to do some juggling, but since I don't do public gigs or anything, that's super fine.
