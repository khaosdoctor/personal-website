Description

This is the throttle half of my HOTAS, VIRPIL's mid-size throttle with two rotating throttle handles on contactless sensors that can run independently or latched together (never used them independently), plus an auxiliary axis on the base and a slider and analogue ministick on the handles.

The base panel has six RGB-backlit buttons, seven toggle switches, two push encoders and a 5-way mode dial that multiplies the button functions, which puts it well over 30 inputs (I don't even use them all). It's quite sturdy, and expensive.
