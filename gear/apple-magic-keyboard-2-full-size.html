Description #

I got it from a company that I don't remember which, I think it was Klarna. Back then I used it as my main keyboard, but it's quite wide since it's a full size keyboard.

Impressions #

The impressions are the same as for the other keyboard. They are really well made, the battery lasts quite long, and the typing feel is very good. I don't have any complaints with it.

It's just that in this case it's not possible to take it with me almost anywhere, because it's very wide and takes almost the entire space in a backpack, so I have to mind what I'm doing with it. I usually just keep it as a memento or some sort of collection item, but I really don't use it anymore.
