Description

A stage tuner pedal that shows whether each string is in tune and mutes the signal while you tune. Chromatic pedal tuner with an 11-segment LED meter and note display; buffered bypass with a mute/tuning mode, and a power-out jack that can daisy-chain power to other pedals from a single 9V center-negative adapter.
