Description

A soldering station heats an iron to melt solder so you can join electronic components, with a base unit that holds a steady temperature for you. This is a 60 W ESD-safe station with PID temperature control and an LED display, adjustable from 90 to 480 °C (°C/°F toggle), reaching about 300 °C in roughly 15 seconds, with auto-sleep after 10 minutes idle and 900M-series tips. The kit came with the iron, a desoldering pump, tweezers, a roll of solder, a brass/sponge tip cleaner, and 6 interchangeable tips.
