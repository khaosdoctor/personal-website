Description

This is the grip of the HOTAS, the original Constellation Alpha on a VPC WarBRD base, and a huge evolution from my other HOTAS.

It's a space-sim oriented grip with a dual-stage trigger, a hinged second trigger, three 4-way hats and a 2-way hat, a brake lever, a scroll encoder and an analogue ministick, all on top of a lockable twist axis, there are a lot of buttons in this thing.

It's quite versatile because I use it to play Elite Dangerous and Euro Truck Simulator 2, which are wildly different games, as you can imagine.
