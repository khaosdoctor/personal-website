E-ink bus times computer

planned updated 2026-10-12

ESP32 with an e-ink screen showing nearby bus and subway departures, with today's weather

Overview #

Good project to learn how to work with ESP32 and hardware using E-Ink screens. The goal here is to learn how to work with systems that need to use an exceptionally small amount of energy.

Design #

  • Small Eink screen to show bus and subway departure times in nearby stations
  • Show tasks I have to do and today's calendar
